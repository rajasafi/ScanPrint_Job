You can pair an HP or Canon monochrome laser printer for black-and-white documents alongside an Epson or Brother ink tank printer for color graphics and photos. When a customer chooses color or monochrome at the QR kiosk, our software automatically dispatches the job to the appropriate machine with zero manual switching.
